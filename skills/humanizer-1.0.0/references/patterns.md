# Humanizer Pattern Checklist

Use this reference only when the rewrite needs a careful pass.

## Inflated Significance

Watch for language that makes ordinary facts sound historically important:

- "stands as", "serves as", "is a testament to"
- "marks a pivotal moment"
- "underscores the importance"
- "plays a crucial role"
- "reflects a broader trend"

Fix by stating the actual function, evidence, or consequence.

## Promotional Tone

Watch for marketing-style language:

- "cutting-edge", "groundbreaking", "revolutionary"
- "seamless", "robust", "powerful", "transformative"
- "unlock", "elevate", "harness", "empower"

Fix by replacing vague praise with observable capability or measured effect.

## Vague Attribution

Watch for claims assigned to blurry groups:

- "experts say"
- "researchers believe"
- "many argue"
- "it is widely recognized"

Fix by naming the source, narrowing the claim, or marking uncertainty.

## Formulaic Structure

Watch for overly balanced or symmetrical phrasing:

- "not only X but also Y"
- "whether X or Y"
- "from X to Y"
- repeated three-item lists
- paragraphs that all end with broad implications

Fix by varying structure and keeping only the contrast that matters.

## Hollow Analysis

Watch for commentary that describes the act of analysis rather than doing it:

- "this highlights the complexity"
- "this raises important questions"
- "this provides valuable insights"
- "this demonstrates the need for further research"

Fix by naming the specific mechanism, limitation, or next decision.

## Generic Transitions

Watch for filler transitions:

- "moreover", "furthermore", "in conclusion"
- "it is important to note"
- "this article explores"
- "in today's rapidly evolving landscape"

Fix by cutting them or replacing them with concrete logical links.

## Rhythm Problems

AI-like prose often has evenly sized sentences and paragraphs. Improve rhythm by:

- splitting over-packed sentences
- combining short repetitive sentences
- moving the strongest clause to the end or beginning
- using plain connective words when they fit

## Academic Writing Guardrails

For academic or technical prose:

- Keep hedging where the evidence requires it.
- Do not add first person unless it fits the genre.
- Keep defined terms stable.
- Keep equations, units, citations, and experimental conditions untouched.
- Replace "novel" or "significant" only when the evidence does not support them.

## Final Pass

Before returning the rewrite, check:

- Does it still mean the same thing?
- Did any sentence become less precise?
- Are claims still supported?
- Does the voice fit the audience?
- Did the edit remove formulaic polish without adding fake personality?
